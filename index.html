LabelLens
Powered by Google Gemma 4 · open-weight

Snap a label. Know what you're eating.

Packaged food labels are tiny, technical and often in English only. Take a photo and LabelLens reads it with Gemma 4's vision, explains every ingredient in plain words and flags what matters for your diet, in English, Hindi or Hinglish.
